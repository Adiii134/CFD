Model & limitations

Solver. A 2-D D2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann (BGK) solver with a Smagorinsky-type sub-grid viscosity for stability. Streamlines are integrated (RK2) through the computed velocity field every other frame; bright pulses travel at the local flow speed.

  • Car: solved on the vertical centre-plane (side view). The spanwise lines are copies of that 2-D solution, blended to free-stream away from the body — a visual approximation, not a 3-D solve.
  • Ground is a free-slip floor (a rough stand-in for a moving belt). The simulated Reynolds number (~10²–10³) is far below the real ~10⁷; both are shown.
  • Cd is a 2-D slice momentum-exchange estimate — indicative only.
  • Water: depth-averaged flow in plan view. Surface height comes from the solver’s pressure field (Bernoulli-like) driving a damped wave equation for ripples, advected by the flow. It is not a free-surface or 3-D solver.
  • Simulated Re (~10²) is far below real water flow (~10⁵); both are shown.

Not a validated engineering CFD tool. Use it to build intuition, not to predict drag or loads. Solver steps per frame adapt to your device.
